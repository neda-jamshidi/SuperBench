# SuperBench

SUPERBENCH is a cross-cultural benchmark for evaluating how Large Language Models (LLMs) understand and reason about superstitions—culturally transmitted beliefs, rituals, taboos, and omens that often connect actions or events to outcomes without empirical justification.

By examining these culturally grounded yet non-empirical beliefs across countries and languages, SUPERBENCH investigates how LLMs represent cultural knowledge, distinguish between cultural contexts, and generalize across cultures and languages.
